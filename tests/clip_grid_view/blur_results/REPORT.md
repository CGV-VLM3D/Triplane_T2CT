## Verdict
Over 64 samples the three generated models are **statistically comparable in global sharpness**:
mean hf_clip 0.0277 / 0.0298 / 0.0281 with std ≈0.013 — differences (~0.002) are far inside one std.
Rankings flip metric-to-metric and mean ranks all sit ≈2.0 (middle of 3) → **no model is consistently
blurriest or sharpest.** The single-sample impression that "wan is blurry" (valid_1_a_1) does NOT
generalize; that case was unrepresentative. Resample to clip_grid removes high-freq mildly and roughly
uniformly across models (ratio 0.78–0.94), so it is not the cause of any per-case blur either.
